# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I get a refund?

Monthly subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of payment if you have
not used more than 100 task updates. Annual subscriptions can be refunded on a
pro-rated basis within 30 days of purchase. Contact support to start a refund.

## How do I reset my password?

Open the sign-in page, select "Forgot password", and enter your work email. We
will send a reset link that expires after 30 minutes. If the email does not
arrive, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Is my data backed up?

Yes. FlowDesk data is backed up every six hours and backups are retained for 30
days. Enterprise customers can request a longer retention period.

## Do you offer discounts?

We offer a 20% discount for registered non-profit organizations and a 50%
discount for students and educators. Discounts cannot be combined with other
offers.

## Which browsers are supported?

FlowDesk supports the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
Internet Explorer is not supported.
