# Northwind Labs

Northwind Labs is a small software company founded in 2019 and based in
Portland, Oregon. We build productivity tools for small teams. Our flagship
product is FlowDesk, a shared workspace for task tracking and team notes.

## Support hours

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pacific Time.
We are closed on public holidays. You can reach us at
support@northwindlabs.example.

## Plans

- Starter: $8 per user per month, up to 10 users.
- Team: $15 per user per month, unlimited users, includes priority support.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, SSO/SAML, audit logs, and a dedicated account
  manager.

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to
start a trial.
